# FinanceGuard - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Start the App
```bash
npm run dev
```

### Step 2: Create Your Account
1. Open http://localhost:5173 in your browser
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email, password, and name
4. Click "Create Account"

### Step 3: Add Sample Data
1. After logging in, click the blue "Add Sample Data" button
2. Wait a few seconds for data to load
3. Explore your dashboard!

## ✨ What You'll See

- **$100,000+ in income** from salary transactions
- **$3,000+ in expenses** across various categories
- **4 budget trackers** showing your spending limits
- **3 alerts** including budget warnings and fraud detection

## 🎯 Try These Features

1. **Add a Transaction**
   - Go to "Transactions" tab
   - Fill in the form and click "Add Transaction"

2. **Set a Budget**
   - In "Overview" tab, click "+" in Budget Tracker
   - Choose a category and amount

3. **View Analytics**
   - Click "Analytics" tab
   - See spending breakdown by category
   - View monthly trends

4. **Check Alerts**
   - Click "Alerts" tab
   - Mark alerts as read

## 🔥 Pro Tips

- Hover over transactions to see the delete button
- Budget tracker shows red when you exceed your limit
- Analytics tab updates automatically as you add transactions
- Use the chatbot icon (bottom right) for help
